# Privacy Policy

Miroji · Last updated: 30 October 2026

> **Short version:** Miroji is a simple mirror. It shows your device's camera feed on screen in real time. It does **not** collect, store, record, or transmit any personal data, photos, or video. Everything happens on your device.

The hosted version of this policy (the URL used in the Google Play and App Store listings) is published at:
**<https://rogeriodocarmo.github.io/mirror_app/privacy-policy.html>**

## 1. Who we are

Miroji ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile application that uses your device's camera (the front-facing camera by default, or the rear camera if you switch) to display a live mirror view. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## 2. Information we collect

**None.** Miroji does not collect, request, or store any personal or sensitive user data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that gather data.

## 3. Camera usage

Miroji requires camera permission for a single purpose: to display your camera feed on the screen as a mirror. It starts on the front camera, and you can switch to the rear camera, zoom in with a pinch, and flip the image left to right. On the rear camera you can also turn on the flashlight, which is simply the light next to your device's camera, switched on and off only by you. The camera feed is processed live, on your device, and is shown only to you. The app does **not** take photos, does **not** record video, does **not** save any frames, and does **not** send the camera feed anywhere. Nothing from the camera ever leaves your device.

## 4. Device language

The app reads your device's language setting locally to display its interface in a supported language (English, Portuguese, Spanish, or Japanese). This preference is read on-device only and is never collected or transmitted.

## 5. A one-time tip

The first time you open Miroji it briefly shows a tip explaining that you can pinch to zoom. To avoid showing it again, the app saves one empty file, named `zoom-hint-seen`, in its own private storage on your device. The file contains nothing, is not personal data, is never read by anyone but the app, and never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall Miroji or clear its data.

## 6. Data sharing and selling

Because Miroji collects no data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

## 7. Data retention and security

Miroji stores no personal data, so there is no user data to retain, delete, or secure on any server. The app operates entirely on your device.

## 8. Children's privacy

Miroji does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads, no purchases, and no data collection.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
